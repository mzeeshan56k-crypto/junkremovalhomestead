County Bulky Pickup Rules in Leisure City

Leisure City is unincorporated, so trash service comes from Miami-Dade County rather than the City of Homestead. The county allows two bulky waste pickups per year, up to 25 cubic yards each. It will not collect single items over 150 pounds, construction debris or tires. Piles can go out no more than 3 days before the appointment, and anything placed early can bring a code citation.

A junk pickup service in Leisure City is the easy fix when your two county pickups are used up. It also helps with heavy items like a fridge or hot tub, or when you cannot wait weeks. We take items straight from wherever they sit and handle disposal and recycling for you.
