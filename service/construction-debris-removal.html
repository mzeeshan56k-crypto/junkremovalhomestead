Construction Debris Removal

Renovation and Construction Cleanup Homestead Builders Trust

Homestead has been one of the fastest growing cities in Miami-Dade County, with new subdivisions, additions and remodels going up every month. Every one of those projects leaves behind debris, and most of it cannot go in a regular trash can. City of Homestead curbside collection does not accept construction debris, and Miami-Dade Trash and Recycling Centers cap construction and demolition waste at 3 cubic yards per visit.

That is where our construction debris removal service helps. Our crew arrives with a 15 cubic yard truck, loads everything by hand, and hauls it the same day. You do not need a dumpster sitting in the driveway for a week, and you do not need to pull your workers off the job to load it.

  • Tile, grout and flooring

  • Doors, windows and frames
  • Carpet, padding and underlayment
  • Insulation and packaging

Remodel Debris Removal for Homeowners

Doing a DIY kitchen or bathroom remodel? A typical kitchen tear out produces 3 to 5 cubic yards of cabinets, counters and drywall, while a full bathroom gut runs 2 to 4 yards. We pick up in one visit, from the garage, driveway or right from the room, and sweep the area when finished. If your project spans several weekends, a dumpster rental may be a better fit.

Job Site Cleanup for Contractors

General contractors, flooring installers, painters and handymen across Homestead and Florida City use us for scheduled pickups between phases. We can meet a super on site at 7 AM, load quickly, and be gone before trades arrive. Final cleanouts before a certificate of occupancy walkthrough are a specialty.

The EPA estimates that about 600 million tons of construction and demolition debris are generated in the United States each year, more than twice the amount of household trash. We separate clean wood, metal and cardboard for recycling whenever practical to keep more of it out of the South Dade Landfill.

Demolition Debris and Small Structure Removal

We also tear down and haul small structures such as sheds, playsets, wooden decks and chain link fences. For concrete, pavers and dirt we quote by weight since these materials are dense. Storm damaged structures are covered by our hurricane debris removal service.

FAQs

Construction Debris Removal FAQs

What builders and homeowners in Homestead ask about debris hauling.

Does the City of Homestead pick up construction debris?

No. City of Homestead curbside collection excludes construction debris, concrete, roofing materials and similar items. Miami-Dade Trash and Recycling Centers accept up to 3 cubic yards per visit, but you must haul it there yourself.

How much does construction debris removal cost in Homestead?

Small piles of 2 to 4 cubic yards typically cost $150 to $280. A full 15 cubic yard truck of mixed debris runs about $560 to $700, depending on weight.

Is it cheaper to rent a dumpster or hire debris removal?

For jobs finished in a day, debris removal is often cheaper because labor is included. For projects lasting a week or more, a dumpster at $295 to $525 is usually the better value.

Do you remove drywall?

Yes. Drywall is one of our most common pickups. It is heavy, roughly 1.6 to 2.2 pounds per square foot for standard half inch board, so large amounts may be priced by weight.

Can you pick up debris the same day?

Yes. Same day construction debris pickup is available in Homestead when you call before noon, and early morning pickups can be scheduled for contractors.

Do you haul concrete and pavers?

Yes. Concrete, pavers, brick and dirt are quoted by weight because they are very dense. A single cubic yard of concrete weighs about 4,000 pounds.

Do you recycle construction waste?

Whenever practical, we separate clean wood, metal and cardboard for recycling. The EPA estimates the US generates about 600 million tons of construction and demolition debris each year.

Can you remove a shed or deck?

Yes. We dismantle and haul sheds, playsets, decks and fences. Pricing depends on size and materials, and we quote after reviewing photos.

Do you offer recurring pickups for contractors?

Yes. We schedule weekly or phase based pickups for builders and remodelers in Homestead, Florida City and Cutler Bay, with simple monthly invoicing.

Will you clean up after the debris is loaded?

Yes. Every job ends with a sweep of the work area, and we can blow off driveways so the site is ready for inspection or the homeowner walkthrough.
