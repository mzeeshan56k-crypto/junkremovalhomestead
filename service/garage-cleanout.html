Garage Cleanout

Garage Junk Removal That Gives You the Space Back

In South Florida, where most homes lack basements and attics are too hot for storage, the garage becomes the catch-all. A packed two-car garage can hold 8 to 15 cubic yards of stuff, the equivalent of half to a full truckload. Many Homestead homeowners have not parked inside in years.

Our garage cleanout service is built for exactly that. We work alongside you to decide what stays, then remove everything else in one visit. Keepers get moved to one side, donations go to local charities, recyclables get separated, and the rest is hauled away. Most garage cleanouts in Homestead take 2 to 4 hours from start to finish.

What We Clear From Homestead Garages

  • Boxes and storage bins
  • Old furniture and mattresses
  • Spare refrigerators and freezers
  • Broken tools and lawn equipment
  • Bikes, toys and sports gear
  • Shelving and workbenches
  • Lumber, tile and remodel leftovers
  • Holiday decorations
  • Old electronics and TVs
  • Exercise equipment

Sort, Donate and Haul in One Visit

We do not just throw everything in the truck. Our crew separates items into keep, donate, recycle and dispose piles based on your direction. Usable goods are delivered to charities in South Miami-Dade, metal goes to scrap recyclers, and electronics go to certified electronics recyclers. That process can divert a large share of a typical garage load from the landfill.

Hazardous Items Found in Garages

Garages often contain old paint, pool chemicals, motor oil, pesticides and propane tanks. We cannot haul those in our trucks, but we will set them aside and point you to Miami-Dade County Home Chemical Collection options. The South Dade Home Chemical Collection Center at 23707 SW 97th Avenue accepts paint, chemicals and used motor oil.

Garage Cleanouts for Moves, Sales and Rentals

Preparing to sell? A clean, empty garage improves listing photos and buyer showings. Moving out of a rental near Homestead Air Reserve Base? We clear the garage before your final walk-through. For whole-house jobs, see our estate cleanout service, and for single bulky items like a spare fridge, our appliance removal team can help.

Why Homestead Garages Fill Up So Fast

Without basements and with attics that top 130 degrees in summer, Homestead homes rely on the garage for everything. Add hurricane supplies, shutters, generators and pool equipment, and the space disappears fast. Summer humidity also leads to mold on cardboard boxes and rust on tools, which makes older clutter worth clearing out.

We recommend leaving room for hurricane shutters and supplies after a cleanout so you are ready well before June 1 each year.

FAQs

Garage Cleanout FAQs

Common questions about clearing a garage in Homestead.

How much does a garage cleanout cost in Homestead?

Most garage cleanouts in Homestead cost between $280 and $650. A full two-car garage usually fills most of our 15-cubic-yard truck and runs $520 to $650.

How long does a garage cleanout take?

Most garage cleanouts take 2 to 4 hours, including sorting, loading and sweeping. Heavily packed garages may take longer, and we give a time estimate with the quote.

Will you help me sort what to keep?

Yes. Our crew works with you to separate keep, donate, recycle and dispose items. You make the calls, and we do the lifting.

Can you take old paint and chemicals?

No. Paint, pool chemicals, oil and pesticides are hazardous. We set them aside and point you to Miami-Dade Home Chemical Collection options.

Do you donate items from garage cleanouts?

Yes. Usable tools, furniture, bikes and household goods are donated to local charities in South Miami-Dade whenever possible.

Can you remove a garage refrigerator?

Yes. Spare refrigerators and freezers are common garage items. We haul them to recyclers that recover refrigerant as required by the Clean Air Act.

Do you remove shelving and workbenches?

Yes. We dismantle shelving units, cabinets and workbenches and remove them along with the rest of the garage contents.

Can you do a garage cleanout the same day?

Yes. Same-day garage cleanouts are often possible when you call before noon. Larger garages may be scheduled for the next morning.

Do I need to be there during the cleanout?

For sorting, it helps to be present. If everything goes, you can provide access, and we will send before-and-after photos.

Can you clean out a storage unit too?

Yes. We clear storage units around Homestead and Florida City using the same volume-based pricing as garage cleanouts.

Do you offer garage cleanout in Homestead ZIP codes 33030, 33032 and 33033?

Yes. We sort, haul and sweep out garages, sheds and carports in each of these ZIP codes. That covers every Homestead ZIP code from 33030 to 33035, plus nearby Goulds, South Miami Heights and Cutler Bay. Pricing is the same in every ZIP code.
