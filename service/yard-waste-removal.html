Yard Debris Removal Homestead Properties Need Year Round

Homestead's tropical climate means plants never really stop growing. With nearly 60 inches of rain each year and warm temperatures in every month, palms, hedges and trees produce a steady flow of fronds, clippings and branches. Most of it piles up behind the shed or along the fence line until it becomes a real project.

Our yard waste removal service clears that pile in one visit. We rake up loose debris, bag small material, load branches and haul it all to a facility that processes vegetation into mulch whenever possible. Farms and nurseries in the Redland and larger lots off Krome Avenue are regular customers too.

Brush Pile Removal and Backyard Cleanups

Overgrown lots and neglected backyards are common with rentals, estates and newly purchased homes. We clear brush piles, pull out dead plants and haul old landscaping so the yard is ready for new sod or a garden. Many cleanups also turn up old furniture, tires and junk, which we can remove at the same time through our junk removal service.

Palm Frond and Tree Trimming Debris Removal

Homeowners who trim their own palms or hire a trimmer who does not haul often end up with a mountain of fronds. Royal palm fronds can reach 10 to 15 feet long and weigh up to 50 pounds each, which makes them hard to bundle for curbside pickup. We cut and load them for you.

According to the EPA, yard trimmings make up about 12 percent of all municipal solid waste in the United States, roughly 35 million tons a year. Clean vegetation is much easier to recycle than mixed trash, so we keep yard waste separate whenever possible.

Storm Debris From the Yard

After a tropical storm or hurricane, limbs and fallen trees can block driveways and damage fences. Our crews respond quickly for yard storm cleanup. For larger storm jobs involving damaged structures or contents, see our hurricane debris removal page.

FAQs

Yard Waste Removal FAQs

Answers for Homestead homeowners dealing with branches, fronds and brush.

How much does yard waste removal cost in Homestead?

Small piles of 10 to 15 bags start around $95 to $150. A full truck of brush and branches typically costs $520 to $650. Heavy items like stumps and soil are priced by weight.

Do you remove palm fronds?

Yes. Palm fronds are one of our most common yard pickups in Homestead. We cut long fronds down to size and load them, so you do not need to bundle them yourself.

Do I need to bag the yard waste?

No. We bring bags, rakes and tarps. Bagging small material yourself can lower the price slightly, but it is never required.

Can you remove tree stumps?

We remove small stumps and roots that have already been cut or dug out. For stump grinding of large trees, we recommend a tree service, and we can haul the grindings afterward.

Where does the yard waste go?

Clean vegetation goes to facilities that grind it into mulch and compost. The EPA reports yard trimmings are about 12 percent of US municipal waste, so recycling them matters.

Do you clear overgrown lots?

Yes. We clear overgrown residential lots and backyards throughout Homestead and the Redland, removing brush, vines, trash and old landscaping.

How fast can you remove yard debris?

Same day pickup is usually available when you call before noon. After storms, demand rises, so booking early helps.

Can you take dirt and sod?

Yes. Old sod, landscape rock and dirt are accepted but priced by weight, since one cubic yard of wet soil can weigh over 2,000 pounds.

Do you work with landscapers?

Yes. Landscapers and tree trimmers in Homestead call us to haul debris so their crews can move to the next job faster.

Can you take yard waste and junk together?

Yes. We can load yard waste and household junk in the same truck. Mixed loads are priced together by volume in one simple quote.
